## 🔬 Interests & Explorations

> **AI Applications, Agents & LLM Reliability**  
> I'm interested in building AI systems that support real tasks, with particular attention to LLM evaluation, hallucination detection, and dependable agent workflows. I want to understand how these systems behave, where they fail, and how to make them more useful and trustworthy.
>
> **Software Engineering & Developer Tools**  
> I enjoy the engineering work that turns an idea into usable software: clear interfaces, useful development tools, automated testing, and observable workflows. I'm especially interested in how AI can support developers and improve engineering processes.

My background also includes computer vision, robotics perception, sensor fusion, and engineering data visualization.

---

## 🌱 What I'm Working Toward

> I want to connect AI ideas with dependable software — understanding both the models and the engineering around them. My focus is on building systems that can be evaluated, tested, maintained, and improved through real use.
